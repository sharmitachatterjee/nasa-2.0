CHRONICLES OF EXPLORATION

Mission Timeline

From the dusty tracks of Apollo on the Moon to Voyager’s silent voyage into interstellar space. Trace the chronology of humanity's boldest robotic emissaries.

AUGUST 1971 · THE MOON

Apollo 15 Lunar Roving Vehicle

The first automobile driven on another celestial body. Dave Scott and Jim Irwin logged 17.3 miles around Hadley Rille before parking the rover to film the lunar liftoff. It remains undisturbed under the lunar vacuum.

SEPTEMBER 1977 · INTERSTELLAR TRAJECTORY

Voyager 1 & 2 Launch

Carrying the Golden Record, Voyager 1 conducted flybys of Jupiter and Saturn before crossing the heliopause into interstellar space in August 2012. It is currently over 24 billion kilometers from Earth.

OCTOBER 1997 · SATURN ORBIT

Cassini-Huygens Mission

Orbited Saturn for 13 years, discovering cryovolcanic geysers on Enceladus and lakes of liquid methane on Titan. In 2017, it completed the "Grand Finale" intentional plunge into Saturn’s atmosphere.

JANUARY 2004 · MARS EXPLORATION ROVERS

Spirit (MER-A) & Opportunity (MER-B)

Twin robotic geologists bounced down in airbags on opposite sides of Mars. Designed for 90 sols, Spirit climbed mountains until 2010; Opportunity endured for 5,111 sols (nearly 15 years) and drove 28.06 miles across Mars.

APRIL 2021 – JANUARY 2024 · MARS AIRSPACE

Ingenuity Mars Helicopter

The first aircraft to achieve powered, controlled flight on another planet. Designed for 5 test flights, the 1.8-kg rotorcraft executed 72 flights across Jezero Crater before rotor damage concluded its aerial mission.
