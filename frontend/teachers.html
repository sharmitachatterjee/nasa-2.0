MODULE 1 · PHYSICS

The Physics of Light Delay

Core Concept: Finite speed of light ($c = 299,792\text{ km/s}$) and deep-space latency.

Student Problem: When Mars is closest to Earth (56 million km), calculate how many minutes a radio command takes to reach Opportunity. If a rover is heading toward a cliff at 5 cm/s, explain why Earth controllers cannot steer it with a live joystick.

→ Recommended Tool: Signal Delay Simulator

MODULE 2 · GEOLOGY & ASTROBIOLOGY

Planetary Geology & Ancient Water

Core Concept: Sedimentary rock analysis, hematite spherules ("blueberries"), and evidence of ancient martian oceans.

Discussion Prompt: How did Opportunity’s discovery of jarosite and sulfate minerals prove that ancient Mars had liquid water with neutral-to-acidic pH? What does this imply about the potential for past microbial life?

→ Recommended Tool: Then & Now Slider

MODULE 3 · INTERSTELLAR ANTHROPOLOGY

The Voyager Golden Record

Core Concept: Communicating across deep time and cosmic civilizations through binary mathematics and atomic constants.

Class Activity: Have students select three contemporary sounds, one song, and one mathematical diagram to represent 21st-century civilization to an extraterrestrial intelligence.

→ Recommended Tool: Ask Voyager 1

MODULE 4 · ETHICS & ORBITAL MECHANICS

Planetary Protection & The Grand Finale

Core Concept: COSPAR planetary protection protocols and intentional spacecraft disposal.

Ethics Debate: Why did NASA choose to destroy the pristine Cassini orbiter in Saturn's atmosphere rather than let it drift in orbit indefinitely? How do Enceladus's geysers justify destroying an expensive spacecraft?

→ Recommended Tool: The Farewell Memorial
